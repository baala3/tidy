Privacy Policy

Tidy, the app, collects nothing. Buying Tidy Pro means your email address is stored so your key can be sent and found again. That's all.

Last updated November 25, 2026. The data controller is Kamandla Balashekhar, India, kamandlabalashekhar@gmail.com.

The app

Tidy has no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or account. What it scans stays on your PC.

Its only connection is a request to GitHub, when it opens and once a day, asking whether a newer version is out. It carries nothing about your PC, though GitHub sees your IP address as it does for any visit. You can turn it off in Settings > Updates. Updating other apps is winget's own work, and Tidy sends nothing to winget's sources.

Your license key is checked on your PC and never sent anywhere. The 7-day trial is a date in Tidy's settings file on your PC.

Buying Tidy Pro

Paddle.com is the reseller and Merchant of Record. It collects your name, email, country, and payment details, and handles them under Paddle's privacy policy. I never see your card details.

When a purchase completes, Paddle tells Tidy's license server, which keeps:

  • your email address and Paddle's order ID,
  • your license key, which has the same email and order ID in it,
  • when it was bought and emailed, and whether it was refunded.

These are kept so you can get your key again and so refunds are handled correctly. The key is emailed through Resend, which sees your address and the email. The server runs on Cloudflare.

Getting your key again

The lost key page sends the address you type to the license server. The server keeps that address and the time for an hour or so, to stop anyone flooding an inbox, and emails the key only if the address bought one.

This website

The site has no cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and loads fonts from Google Fonts and scripts from cdnjs. Like any web host, they see your IP address and browser. The checkout is Paddle's, and only loads when you choose Buy.

Your rights

Write to kamandlabalashekhar@gmail.com to see, correct or delete what the license server holds about you. Deleting it means your key can't be sent again, but the key you have keeps working. Records Paddle must keep for tax law stay with Paddle.

Nothing is sold or shared for advertising, ever.
